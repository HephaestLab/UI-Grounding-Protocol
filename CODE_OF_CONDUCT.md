# Code of Conduct

UGP contributors are expected to make participation respectful, inclusive, and
free from harassment. Be considerate, critique ideas rather than people, assume
good intent while addressing concrete harm, and respect privacy.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory language, threats,
unwelcome sexual attention, deliberate intimidation, sustained disruption, or
publishing another person's private information without permission.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently
restrict participation when behavior threatens a healthy project community.
Reports should be made privately through the repository owner's published
contact channel. Maintainers will protect reporter confidentiality to the extent
practical and will respond proportionately.

This policy applies in project spaces and when representing the project in
public spaces.
